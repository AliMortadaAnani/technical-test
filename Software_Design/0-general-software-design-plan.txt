//this software design (or software engineering template) is based on many advices from many resources (university-google-ai models-youtube videos-...)
		
		1- Problem Definition : what is the problem we are trying to solve?
		
		2- WWWWWH 
		--> who, what, when, where, why, how

		i) who will use the solution (client : users(admins),customers,...)
		ii) what will the solution do (features)
		iii) when the solution is needed (mvp features vs later features ~ phases and deadlines)
		iv) where will the solution be used (platforms: desktop,mobile,...)
		v) why the solution is needed : what will it solves primarily (from problem definition)
		vi) how the solution is made (if client knows what he wants specifically
		or development team decides the appropriate stack & architecture)
		
		3- Business Requirements : what client hopes to achieve (High-level project's goals)
		---> here we dive more into what the client expect as overall from this solution	
		
		4- Stakeholders Requirements : -----> who are the stakeholders 
	    & what how they are related/affected by the solution
		
	
		5- Brainstorming Ideas
		generate many feature ideas as we can without focusing on its scope,realism...

		6- Functional Requirements : specify the things the application should do
		---> external and internal services
		---> system does ... / user does ...
		
		   
		   Non Functional Requirements : specify performance,reliability,security 
		  (benchmarks)
		  
		
		7-  Adapt the MOSCOW methodologie:
			MUST - SHOULD - COULD - WONT
			M:for MVP (least functional state) 
			S:to be implemented for full product (complete product)  
			C:to be re-checked for potential additional features (enhanced product)
			W:to be likely omitted ~ perfectionist features ( out of scope at the time)

		8- Limitations (what problems to expect ~ deal with ~ accept ~ improve for later version)
		
		9- High level and Low level design : specify the user/system interactions
		===> here we set the ERD, database schema, dtos, endpoints of the api,...(very open to change)
		
		10- Development phase && Continious review of the SE plan 
		
		Later come Testing, Deployment, Maintenance
