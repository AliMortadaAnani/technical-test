2- WWWWWH 
		--> who, what, when, where, why, how

		i) who will use the solution (client : users(admins),customers,...)
		ii) what will the solution do (features)
		iii) when the solution is needed (mvp features vs later features ~ phases and deadlines)
		iv) where will the solution be used (platforms: desktop,mobile,...)
		v) why the solution is needed : what will it solves primarily (from problem definition)
		vi) how the solution is made (if client knows what he wants specifically
		or development team decides the appropriate stack & architecture)
